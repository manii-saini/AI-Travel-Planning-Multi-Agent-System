## 5. Tips & Booking Advice  

1. **Book Early** – 45–60 days before travel for flight and hotel discounts.  
2. **Use Local Apps** – Ola, Uber, or local taxi apps for shared rides; they often offer better rates than hotel‑arranged cabs.  
3. **Pack Light** – Carry a small backpack for day‑treks; many hotels offer luggage storage.  
4. **Cash & Cards** – Most hotels accept cards, but keep some cash for small vendors.  
5. **Weather Check** – 2026 peak season (May–June) can see higher prices; check local weather forecasts.  
6. **Travel Insurance** – Recommended for trekking activities.
